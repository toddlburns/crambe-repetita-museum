What this is

Crambe Repetita Visual Inspiration is a personal, non-commercial reference archive. It collects graphic design and images its owner finds visually interesting — record sleeves, posters, book jackets, photographs — so they can be looked at, compared and searched in one place. Nothing is sold here. There is no advertising, no affiliate linking and no paid access.

Nothing here is my work

Every image in this archive was made by someone else. The archive makes no claim of authorship and no claim of ownership over any image it displays. Copyright and all other rights in each work remain with the artist, designer, photographer, publisher, estate or other rights holder. Where a creator is known, the archive names them; where the creator is not established, it says so rather than guessing.

Every item names its source

Each item records where its image came from. Where a public page for the object exists — a museum record, a Discogs release, a gallery or seller listing, a published article — the item links straight to it. Where no such page is available, the source is named in words. Where the origin genuinely could not be established, the item says that too. Source links are never invented to fill a gap.

Images are held at reference size

Images are kept at the resolution needed to see a design clearly and no more. Many are small. They are here as a record of what a thing looks like, with attribution and context — not as a substitute for the original object, a print, or a licensed reproduction.

If you hold rights in something here

If you are a rights holder and would like an image removed, corrected or better credited, please say so and it will be dealt with promptly and without argument. Removal requests are honoured on request; no justification is required.

Corrections

Attribution errors are taken seriously. If something is credited to the wrong person, or a work is misidentified, please get in touch: it will be fixed and the correction noted on the item.
